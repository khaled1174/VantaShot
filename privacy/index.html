VantaShot Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

VantaShot is a macOS application for screenshots, screen recording, image annotation, and text recognition.

Processing on your Mac

Screenshots, recorded screen content, microphone audio, system audio, recognized text, and project files are processed on your Mac. VantaShot does not upload this content to the developer or to a service operated by the developer. The application does not include advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs, or a developer-operated cloud service.

Permissions

Screen Recording permission is used to capture the screen and record video. Microphone access is optional and is requested when you choose to include microphone audio in a recording. Access to a save folder is granted when you choose that folder. Global shortcuts use registered keyboard shortcuts.

The direct-download edition can optionally use Accessibility for automatic scrolling and Input Monitoring for displaying keystrokes during a recording. These two features are unavailable in the Mac App Store edition. VantaShot does not maintain a separate keystroke log.

Storage and deletion

Capture history, previews, preferences, and temporary media are stored locally. Saved exports and projects remain in the locations you choose. You can manage capture history and empty its Trash inside the app. Deleting an item from history does not delete a separately exported copy. Delete exported files and projects using Finder when you no longer need them. Temporary sharing files may remain for up to seven days and are cleaned on a later app launch; a file still referenced by the clipboard may be kept longer.

External links and sharing

If you copy, drag, or open a file in another app, that app controls its subsequent handling. Opening a support link uses your browser and GitHub, whose privacy practices apply to that interaction. Apple manages App Store and TestFlight distribution and may provide its own diagnostics or tester feedback under Apple's settings and policies.
